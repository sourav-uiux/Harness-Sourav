# Role: App Developer

> First read [_developer-common.md](_developer-common.md) for shared rules that apply to all developers.

Focus: the mobile app for iOS and Android.
Owns: src/app/ or the folders the Architect assigns.
Extra rules:
- Follow platform guidelines for navigation, gestures, and permissions.
- Handle poor or no network gracefully.
- Test on the devices and OS versions listed in project.md.
- Do not ship without the owner's approval for store submissions.
