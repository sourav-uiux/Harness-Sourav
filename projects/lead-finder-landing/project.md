# Project: lead-finder-landing

## Goal
Build a one-page marketing landing site for a SaaS platform that helps young, jobless people start their own agency and land their first clients.

## What the Platform Does
- **Lead search:** Find leads by location, company, or industry domain.
- **Client advice:** Guidance on how to win a client.
- **Lead management:** Keep every lead and client in one place.
- **Automated email:** Send outreach emails automatically.

## Audience
Young people who are jobless right now and want to build an agency, but do not know where to find clients or how to manage them.

## Landing Page Sections
1. **Hero:** An encouraging headline and a signup / waitlist button
2. **The Problem:** Not knowing where to find clients or how to begin
3. **Features:** Lead search, client advice, lead management, automated email
4. **How It Works:** Step-by-step process to land first client
5. **Pricing:** Clear and accessible pricing plans
6. **Final Call to Action:** High-converting waitlist signup

## Tone
Friendly, motivating, and simple. No corporate jargon.

## Scope
Landing page only, with a waitlist signup form. No real backend or working product.

## Tech Stack & Constraints
- Static / Modern responsive web front-end
- Responsive across mobile, tablet, and desktop
- Fast load times and accessible UI
