## 3. Section 2: The Problem ("Why It Feels Hard Right Now")
- **Heading:** `"Starting an agency sounds great until you ask: 'Where do I find clients?'"`
- **Subhead:** *"Most people give up before they even start because of three big obstacles:"*
- **3 Problem Cards:**
  1. **"The Blind Search:"** Scrolling Google Maps or Instagram for hours with no idea who actually needs your service.
  2. **"The Pitch Panic:"** Staring at an empty email draft, terrified of sounding spammy or getting rejected.
  3. **"The Spreadsheets Nightmare:"** Losing track of who you messaged, who replied, and when to follow up.

---

## 4. Section 3: The Features ("Everything You Need to Win Your First Deal")
- **Heading:** `"Your Entire Agency Toolkit in One Simple Dashboard"`
- **4 Feature Showcase Cards:**
  1. **🎯 Instant Lead Search:**
     - *Copy:* Search local businesses by city, company name, or industry. Get verified owner emails, phone numbers, and websites in seconds.
  2. **💡 Step-by-Step Client Advice:**
     - *Copy:* Don't know what to say? Access battle-tested cold pitch templates, discovery question guides, and pricing calculators proven to close deals.
  3. **📋 Visual Lead & Client Tracker:**
     - *Copy:* A Kanban-style CRM designed for beginners. Move leads from *New Lead* → *Contacted* → *Meeting Booked* → *Paid Client*.
  4. **🤖 Automated Outreach Email:**
     - *Copy:* Send warm, personalized messages without copy-pasting for hours. Automatic follow-ups that turn cold leads into interested replies.

---

## 5. Section 4: How It Works ("From Zero to Your First $1,000 Client in 4 Steps")
- **Step 1:** **Pick Your Niche** — Choose a service you enjoy (web design, social media, SEO, video editing) and pick a target industry.
- **Step 2:** **Generate Your Lead List** — Click search to discover 50+ local businesses in your city that need your service.
- **Step 3:** **Send Guided Pitch Messages** — Use our built-in friendly email scripts to reach out with zero stress.
- **Step 4:** **Deliver and Get Paid** — Manage your active projects and get paid directly by your new happy client.

## 7. Section 6: Final Call to Action & Waitlist Form
- **Heading:** `"Ready to stop waiting for a job and build your own income?"`
- **Subhead:** *"Join our early access waitlist today and get our free 'First Client in 14 Days' starter playbook delivered immediately."*
- **Form Card:**
  - Input 1: `Your Name` (text)
  - Input 2: `Your Best Email` (email, required)
  - Dropdown / Selector: `What service do you want to offer?`
    - Web Design & Development
    - Social Media Management
    - Content Creation / Video
    - Lead Generation
    - Not sure yet / Exploring
  - Submit Button: `Get Early Access & Free Playbook →`

    > *"🎉 You're on the VIP waitlist! We've saved your spot. Check your email shortly for your complimentary First-Client Starter Kit."*
